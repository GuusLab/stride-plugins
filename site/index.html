Announcement Bar

One clear announcement bar at the top of every page

marketing v1.0.0 · GuusLab

About Announcement Bar

Announcement Bar puts one short message at the very top of every published page: a sale, a holiday closing, a new product or an event. Type it in the site settings and it is live; empty the message and the bar is gone.

Add an optional link, such as "Shop the roast", to a page on your site or any https address. Pick a style that suits your theme: Quiet (dark), Loud (blue), Warm (orange) or your own colour, with text that switches between light and dark by itself to stay readable.

Let visitors close it if you like. A closed bar stays closed in that visitor's browser until you change the message, so a new announcement always gets seen.

It is small and respectful: a few lines of CSS, a tiny script only when closing is enabled, no fonts, images, tracking or third-party requests. The bar is a labelled region with a real, keyboard-focusable close button and escaped text.

It needs only the storage permission, for its settings. Without it, pages are served unchanged.

Cookie Consent

An accessible GDPR cookie banner in Dutch or English, with no third parties

privacy v1.0.0 · GuusLab

About Cookie Consent

Cookie Consent adds a clean, accessible cookie banner to every published page. Visitors can accept everything, reject everything with one equally prominent click, or open the settings and choose per category: necessary, analytics and marketing.

The banner speaks the page's language: Dutch pages get Dutch text, all others English. Write your own text for either language, link your privacy policy, pick a floating card or a full-width bar, and set the background, text and button colours. Button text stays readable on any colour you choose.

The choice is remembered for 180 days in a first-party cookie, so returning visitors never see the banner flash. A small Cookie settings button, or any element with data-cookie-settings, lets visitors change their mind.

Other scripts can wait for consent: the html element carries data-cookie-consent (for example "necessary analytics"), a stride:consent event fires on document with the choice, and window.StrideConsent offers get() and open().

Everything is inline and about 4 KB. No external requests, no tracking of its own. It needs only the storage permission for its settings, and still shows its default banner if that permission is refused.

Custom Code

Add analytics, verification tags and widgets to every page. No theme edits.

developeranalytics v1.0.0 · GuusLab

About Custom Code

Custom Code puts your own snippets into the published pages of your site: in the head, right after the opening body tag, or at the end of the body. It is the easy way to add a privacy-friendly analytics script, a search engine verification tag, a web font, a tag manager or a chat widget.

Choose which pages get the code: every page, only the pages you list, or every page except those. A * at the end of an address covers every page under it, so blog/* means all your posts under blog.

Only paste code from people you trust: it runs for every visitor. The plugin asks for one permission, storage, to keep your snippets. Changes apply to a page the next time it is published.

External Links

Links to other sites open in a new tab, safely, with a small arrow.

seoutilities v1.0.0 · GuusLab

About External Links

External Links takes care of every link on your site that points to another website. It opens them in a new tab, so visitors do not lose your page, and adds rel="noopener noreferrer" so the other site cannot reach back into yours.

A small arrow after the link shows readers they are about to leave, and screen readers hear "opens in a new tab". You can also ask search engines not to follow the links (nofollow), or mark them as paid or affiliate links (sponsored).

Your own pages are never touched: links within your site are left as they are, and the plugin picks up your site's domain when you publish. You can also list your other domains, such as a separate shop, so they count as your own. Links you already set to open in a certain way keep that, and domains you name, such as youtube.com, are left completely alone.

Everything happens once, when a page is published: there is no JavaScript on your pages. The plugin asks for one permission, storage, to keep its settings; refused, it uses the defaults.

It needs no setup. Pages with at least three headings get a quiet box titled "On this page" in your site's own fonts and colours. Short pages, the header and the footer are left alone. Headings that already have an id keep it, and duplicate headings get unique anchors.

Prefer it out of the way? Switch to the sticky sidebar style. On wide screens the list stays in the left margin while the reader scrolls, and on smaller screens it falls back to the box. You can also number the sections, list only h2 headings, change the title, pick an accent colour, set the minimum number of headings and skip pages by slug.

The published page gets no JavaScript and makes no third-party requests. The links are plain anchors, the styling is about 1.5 KB of inline CSS, and smooth scrolling only happens for visitors who have not asked for reduced motion. The markup is an accessible nav landmark with a label and visible focus styles.

The only permission it asks for is storage, to keep its settings. Refuse it and the plugin still works with the defaults.

marketing v1.1.0 · GuusLab

About WhatsApp Chat

WhatsApp Chat adds a round WhatsApp button to the corner of every published page. One tap opens a chat with your number, with a message already typed that the visitor can still change.

Place it bottom right or bottom left, keep WhatsApp green or use your own colour, and add a short label such as "Questions? Chat with us". On phones the label hides itself so the button never covers your content.

Optionally show the button only during opening hours: choose the days, the times and your time zone, and visitors outside those hours simply see no button.

It is built to be light and respectful: a plain link to wa.me, no WhatsApp scripts, no tracking and no third-party requests. The pulse and hover animations are pure CSS and switch off for visitors who prefer reduced motion. The button is a real, keyboard-focusable link with a clear accessible name and a contrast-checked icon.

It needs only the storage permission, for its settings.
